FarmGuard system guide

How the crop monitoring and inspection prioritization workflow operates.

System purpose

FarmGuard records crop observations, assigns a rule-based severity level, calculates an urgency score, and prioritizes field inspections. It is designed as an organized inspection aid for farm owners and workers.

Severity classification rules

Low

Minor and stable observation affecting 5% or less of the crop area.

Moderate

Moderate condition, affected area above 5%, or a recurring observation.

High

Significant condition, increasing symptoms, or affected area above 20%.

Critical

Severe plant condition, rapidly spreading symptoms, or affected area above 40%.

Urgency score formula

0.50 × Severity Score + 0.30 × Affected Area + 0.20 × Recurrence Score

Severity values: Low = 25, Moderate = 50, High = 75, Critical = 100. Recurrence is capped at five occurrences and normalized to a 0–100 scale.

Data Structures and Algorithms

Max Heap

Ranks pending inspection reports by urgency score.

Tree ADT

Represents the farm → crop → section hierarchy.

Linked List

Maintains a sequential observation history stream.

Hash Table

Indexes crop records for lookup during request processing.

Merge Sort

Sorts observation history records for display.

Important limitation: These are transparent prototype inspection-triage rules, not validated crop-disease standards. FarmGuard does not diagnose diseases or prescribe treatments. Consult a qualified agriculture adviser before using thresholds for real-world decisions.
