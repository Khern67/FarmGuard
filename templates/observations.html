How classification works

FarmGuard evaluates the reported field conditions using transparent, rule-based thresholds.

Low

Minor and stable; affected area is 5% or less.

Moderate

Moderate condition, area above 5%, or recurrence.

High

Significant condition, increasing symptoms, or area above 20%.

Critical

Severe condition, rapid spread, or area above 40%.

This is an inspection prioritization aid, not a crop disease diagnosis or treatment recommendation.
